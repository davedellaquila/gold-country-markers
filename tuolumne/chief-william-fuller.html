Gold Country · Tuolumne County

Chief William Fuller

E Clampus VitusTuolumne County

Where it stands

At the intersection of Sonora Pass Highway (State Highway 108) and Pela Road, on the left when traveling east on Sonora Pass Highway; marker is in a small island formed by the Sonora Pass Highway, Pela Road and Pela Road Cut-off

Coordinates: 38.066517, -120.189683  ·  View on map →

Erected by: Matuca Chapter No. 1849

The words on the plaque

In respect to the memory of Chief William Fuller, 1873 – 1958, who succeeded to the chieftainship of the Mi-Wuk tribe at the age of 15 in 1888 and until his death continued his great interest in the welfare of California Indians. A rancher, logger, rancheria chairman, weather prophet, oracle and chairman of the California Indian Federation. He was revered by all who knew him. The streets Mi-Wuk Village today bear the names he placed upon them. Dedicated this 19th of October 1963 by Chief Fuller's brother[s] in Matuca Chapter, E Clampus Vitus. Erected 1963 by E Clampus Vitus, Matuca Chapter No. 1849.
