Kennedy Mine Headframe

E Clampus VitusAmador County

Where it stands

Reached from Kennedy Mine Road, on the left when traveling east; marker is located at the Kennedy Gold Mine and can be reached via Highway 49 (fee area); at or near 12594 Kennedy Mine Road, Jackson CA 95642

Coordinates: 38.367700, -120.780633  ·  View on map →

Erected by: James W. Marshall Chapter #49

The words on the plaque

Gold changed an entire way of life in California during the Gold Rush. In 1860 Andrew Kennedy and three partners filed 4 mining claims of about 600 feet long. Two shafts were started near what is now Highway 49. The shafts were started, which eventually became the North and South Shafts. The East Shaft, a vertical shaft, 135 foot steel headframe, was built replacing the original wooden headframe which burned down in 1928.…
